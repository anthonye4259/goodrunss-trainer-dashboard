# 🌟 GOODRUNSS AMBASSADOR PROGRAM - COMPLETE!

## 🎯 **OVERVIEW**

A **three-tier community program** that turns your best users into brand advocates, content creators, and facility monitors. This creates a self-sustaining ecosystem where users help grow and improve GoodRunss while earning rewards!

---

## 👥 **THE THREE ROLES**

### 1. 🎾 **COURT CAPTAINS** - Facility Data Experts

**What They Do:**
- Monitor specific facilities/courts
- Report real-time conditions (crowd, skill level, maintenance needs)
- Own 1-5+ facilities depending on tier
- Become the "local expert" for their facilities

**How They're Selected:**
- Apply through app
- Must have 20+ facility reports with 4.5+ quality score
- Admin approval required
- Auto-promoted from reporting system

**Perks by Tier:**

| Tier | Requirements | Free Bookings/Month | Discount | Priority Booking |
|------|-------------|---------------------|----------|------------------|
| 🥉 Bronze | 20 reports, 4.0 quality, 1 facility | 1 free | 10% off | ✅ |
| 🥈 Silver | 50 reports, 4.5 quality, 3 facilities | 3 free | 20% off | ✅ |
| 🥇 Gold | 100 reports, 4.8 quality, 5+ facilities | 5 free | 30% off | ✅ |

**Example:**
> Sarah is a Gold Court Captain who monitors 5 tennis courts in Austin. She gets 5 free bookings/month + 30% off all bookings + priority access. She's the first to know about court conditions and helps keep her community informed.

---

### 2. 📸 **UGC CREATORS** - Content Creators

**What They Do:**
- Post videos, photos, reviews of facilities/trainers
- Create drills, tips, tutorials
- Generate engaging content that drives bookings
- Get featured on app homepage

**How They're Selected:**
- Apply through app with portfolio
- Must have 5+ pieces of content with 1000+ views
- Content moderated before approval
- Admin approval required

**Perks by Tier:**

| Tier | Requirements | Commission Rate | Pro Subscription | Featured | Verified |
|------|-------------|----------------|------------------|----------|----------|
| 🥉 Bronze | 5 content, 1k views, 5% engagement | 5% | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| 🥈 Silver | 15 content, 5k views, 7.5% engagement | 10% | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| 🥇 Gold | 30 content, 15k views, 10% engagement | 15% | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

**Commission System:**
- Earn % of bookings generated by your content
- Creator posts video of facility → User books facility → Creator gets 5-15% commission
- Tracked automatically via content engagement

**Example:**
> Marcus is a Silver UGC Creator who posts pickleball drill videos. His latest video got 8k views and generated 47 bookings. He earned $235 (10% commission) and got a free Pro subscription ($25/month value).

---

### 3. 🌟 **AMBASSADORS** - Brand Representatives

**What They Do:**
- Promote GoodRunss in their community
- Host events (clinics, tournaments, meetups)
- Refer new users with unique code
- Represent GoodRunss at local sports events

**How They're Selected:**
- Apply through app with social media presence
- Must have 10+ referrals with 50%+ conversion
- Admin approval required
- Interview process

**Perks by Tier:**

| Tier | Requirements | Commission Rate | Swag Tier | Event Budget |
|------|-------------|----------------|-----------|--------------|
| 🥉 Bronze | 10 refs, 30% conversion, 0 events | 10% | Basic | $0 |
| 🥈 Silver | 25 refs, 40% conversion, 2 events | 15% | Premium | $200/event |
| 🥇 Gold | 50 refs, 50% conversion, 5 events | 20% | Elite | $500/event |

**Referral System:**
- Get unique code (e.g., `GRXYZ123`)
- Earn 10-20% of referred user's first-year bookings
- Tracked automatically
- Paid out monthly

**Event Budget:**
- Use to host community events
- Book facilities, buy food/drinks, get swag
- Report results (signups, attendance, revenue)

**Example:**
> Jenna is a Gold Ambassador in Chicago. She hosts monthly pickleball clinics, has referred 73 users (earning her $2,847 this year), and gets $500/event budget. She's a local celebrity in the pickleball community!

## 🗄️ **DATABASE STRUCTURE**

### **Core Tables:**
1. `program_roles` - 3 roles (Court Captain, UGC Creator, Ambassador)
2. `role_tiers` - 9 tiers (3 per role: Bronze, Silver, Gold)
3. `ambassador_applications` - Application system
4. `program_members` - Active members
5. `program_activity` - Activity tracking
6. `program_rewards` - Earnings/payouts

### **Role-Specific Tables:**

**Court Captains:**
- `court_captains` - Facility assignments, stats, perks

**UGC Creators:**
- `ugc_creators` - Creator profiles
- `ugc_content` - Submitted content (videos, photos, reviews)

**Ambassadors:**
- `ambassadors` - Ambassador profiles, codes
- `ambassador_referrals` - Tracked referrals
- `ambassador_events` - Hosted events

## 📱 **USER FLOW**

### **For Court Captains:**

1. **User reports facilities consistently** (using existing reporting system)
2. **Auto-notification: "You're eligible to become a Court Captain!"**
3. **Apply:** Answer questions, select facilities
4. **Admin reviews:** Check quality score, consistency
5. **Approved:** Assigned facilities, get perks
6. **Daily:** Report conditions, earn credits, level up
7. **Monthly:** Get free bookings, discounts

### **For UGC Creators:**

1. **User posts content** (videos, photos, reviews)
2. **Notice in app: "Become a UGC Creator and earn commission!"**
3. **Apply:** Submit portfolio, social links
4. **Admin reviews:** Check content quality, engagement
5. **Approved:** Get creator badge, commission tracking
6. **Create:** Post drills, tips, facility reviews
7. **Earn:** Get commission on bookings generated
8. **Level up:** More content → Higher tier → Higher commission

### **For Ambassadors:**

1. **User refers friends consistently**
2. **Auto-notification: "Become an Ambassador!"**
3. **Apply:** Submit social media, explain why
4. **Interview:** Admin calls to discuss
5. **Approved:** Get unique code, swag, event budget
6. **Promote:** Share code on social media, host events
7. **Earn:** Commission on referrals, event results tracked
8. **Level up:** More referrals → Higher tier → More commission + budget

**UGC Creators:**
- Cost: 5-15% commission on bookings
- Value: Engaging content that drives bookings (CAC reduction)
- **ROI: 3-5x** (vs. paid ads)

**Ambassadors:**
- Cost: 10-20% commission on referrals
- Value: New user acquisition (typically $50-200 CAC)
- **ROI: 2-5x** (vs. paid acquisition)

**Total Impact:**
- 100 Court Captains: $20k/year cost, $60k value = **$40k net gain**
- 50 UGC Creators: $15k/year commission, $75k booking revenue = **$60k net gain**
- 25 Ambassadors: $25k/year commission, $125k user acquisition value = **$100k net gain**
- **Total: $200k annual value for $60k cost = 3.3x ROI**
