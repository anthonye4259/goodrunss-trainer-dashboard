## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Let them drive halfway through**
   - "What would you like to see next?"
   - Makes it interactive, not a lecture

2. **Use their actual data (if possible)**
   - Pre-load with their client names
   - Makes it feel real, not generic

3. **Show the money**
   - Always circle back to ROI
   - "This feature saves you 5 hours/week = $250/week in billable time"

4. **Create urgency (but don't be pushy)**
   - "We're capping founding members at 500 trainers"
   - "Prices go up after launch in [timeframe]"

5. **Record the demo**
   - "Mind if I record this? I'll send you the video afterward"
   - They can rewatch and share with partners

6. **End with a clear next step**
   - Never end with "Let me know what you think"
   - Always: "Let's get you set up right now" or "How does a 30-day trial sound?"
