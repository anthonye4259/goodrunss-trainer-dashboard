```
Here's a 90-minute golf practice routine:

1. WARM-UP (10 min)
   - Shoulder rotations with club
   - Torso twists (20 each direction)
   - Hip circles
   - Light practice swings increasing gradually

2. SHORT GAME PRACTICE (30 min)
   
   PUTTING (15 min)
   - Gate drill: 20 putts (3 feet)
   - Circle drill: 5 balls from 3, 6, 9 feet
   - Lag putting: 10 putts from 30-40 feet
   - Focus: Pace control and starting line
   
   CHIPPING (15 min)
   - Bump-and-run: 10 chips (7-iron)
   - Standard chip: 10 chips (sand wedge)
   - Flop shot: 5 chips (lob wedge)
   - Vary distances: 10, 20, 30 yards

3. RANGE WORK (40 min)
   
   WEDGES (15 min)
   - 56° wedge: 10 shots to 75 yards
   - 52° wedge: 10 shots to 100 yards
   - Focus: Consistent yardage, solid contact
   
   MID IRONS (15 min)
   - 8-iron: 10 shots
   - 6-iron: 10 shots
   - Focus: Ball-first contact, divot after ball
   
   LONG GAME (10 min)
   - 3-wood: 8 shots
   - Driver: 10 shots
   - Focus: Tempo, balance, center contact

4. PRESSURE PRACTICE (10 min)
   - Play imaginary holes on the range
   - Driver → 6-iron → wedge → putt
   - Create consequences for misses

PRACTICE POINTS:
✓ Always have a target
✓ Quality over quantity
✓ Practice with purpose (not just hitting balls)
✓ Work short game 60% of practice time
✓ Track your progress (write down distances, stats)

POST-PRACTICE:
- Write down 3 things you did well
- Note 1-2 things to work on next session
```

## 📊 KEY DIFFERENCES

### Terminology Changes

| Specialty | Session | Exercise | Volume | Client |
|-----------|---------|----------|--------|--------|
| **Generic Fitness** | Workout | Exercise | Sets x Reps | Client |
| **Basketball** | Practice | Drill | Rounds | Player |
| **Yoga** | Class | Pose/Asana | Breaths/Holds | Student |
| **Pilates** | Class | Movement | Repetitions | Client |
| **Boxing** | Training | Combination | Rounds | Fighter |
| **Running** | Run | Interval | Sets | Runner |
| **Golf** | Lesson/Practice | Drill | Swings | Golfer |

### Content Focus Changes

| Specialty | Equipment | Focus Areas | Language Style |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Generic** | Dumbbells, gym | Sets, reps, form | Motivational, gym-bro |
| **Basketball** | Ball, court, cones | Court drills, vertical jump, conditioning | Sport-specific, game-focused |
